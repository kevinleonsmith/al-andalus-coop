# Al-Andalus: Shadows of the Reconquista
## A Historical RPG Set in Medieval Iberia (711-1492 CE)

## Game Concept Overview
*Al-Andalus: Shadows of the Reconquista* is a character-driven historical RPG set during the centuries of Moorish rule in the Iberian Peninsula. Players will navigate the complex political, religious, and cultural landscape of medieval Spain through the eyes of a customizable protagonist whose choices shape both their personal story and potentially the course of history.

## Character Creation & Development

### Character Origins
Players choose from diverse backgrounds that shape their starting abilities, social connections, and initial storylines:

- **Moorish Noble** - Born to privilege in Córdoba or Granada with education and resources, but caught in political intrigue
- **Christian Knight** - Serving a northern kingdom with martial training but facing religious tensions
- **Sephardic Scholar** - A Jewish intellectual with access to multiple communities but vulnerable to persecution
- **Berber Mercenary** - A skilled fighter from North Africa with outsider status but freedom of movement
- **Andalusian Merchant** - A trader of mixed heritage with economic connections but split loyalties
- **Mozarab Artisan** - A Christian living under Moorish rule with cultural flexibility but complex identity

### Character Attributes
- **Martial** - Combat skill, tactics, and athletic prowess
- **Learning** - Scientific knowledge, languages, and reasoning
- **Faith** - Religious devotion, theological understanding, and spiritual perception
- **Diplomacy** - Social influence, persuasion, and court etiquette
- **Stewardship** - Resource management, economics, and organizational skill
- **Intrigue** - Deception, espionage, and manipulation

### Skill Progression
- Skills develop through use rather than abstract experience points
- Specialized skill trees based on your character's path:
  - **Warrior Path**: Combat specializations (cavalry, swordplay, archery, siege warfare)
  - **Scholar Path**: Sciences (astronomy, medicine, mathematics, alchemy)
  - **Religious Path**: Theological traditions (Islamic jurisprudence, Christian monasticism, Jewish mysticism)
  - **Courtier Path**: Political maneuvering (espionage, rhetoric, romance, etiquette)
  - **Merchant Path**: Trade and craft (navigation, bargaining, crafting, resource management)

## Game World & Setting

### Time Periods (Choose Your Starting Era)
1. **The Conquest (711-756)** - Turbulent period of initial Moorish conquest
2. **Umayyad Golden Age (756-1031)** - Height of Al-Andalus cultural and scientific achievement
3. **Taifa Period (1031-1086)** - Fragmentation and political intrigue
4. **Almoravid & Almohad Rule (1086-1212)** - North African dynasties and religious reform
5. **Late Reconquista (1212-1492)** - Christian kingdoms ascendant, last Moorish strongholds

### Major Locations
- **Córdoba** - Seat of the Caliphate, intellectual center with the Great Mosque and massive library
- **Granada** - Last Moorish kingdom, home to the Alhambra palace and diverse population
- **Toledo** - Former Visigothic capital, center of translation and knowledge exchange
- **Seville** - Major port city with architectural marvels like the Giralda
- **Santiago de Compostela** - Christian pilgrimage site and symbolic heart of the Reconquista
- **Rural regions** where common people navigate changing powers and maintain daily life

### Factions & Allegiances
Players can align with various factions:
- **Political Powers**: Umayyad Caliphate, Taifa kingdoms, Almoravids, Christian realms
- **Religious Orders**: Sufi brotherhoods, monastic orders, rabbinic circles
- **Social Groups**: Merchant guilds, artisan collectives, warrior bands
- **Secret Societies**: Esoteric study circles, political conspirators, religious reformers

## Gameplay Systems

### Dialogue & Relationships
- Extensive dialogue trees with persuasion mechanics influenced by skills and reputation
- Dynamic relationship system tracking your standing with individuals and factions
- Language system where knowing Arabic, Romance dialects, or Hebrew opens different opportunities

### Combat System
- Tactical combat system reflecting period warfare
- Options for direct combat, commanding troops, or avoiding violence entirely
- Historical weapons, armor, and fighting styles from both Muslim and Christian traditions

### Knowledge & Discovery
- Research system drawing on the period's scientific advancements
- Collect manuscripts, conduct experiments, and learn from scholars
- Apply knowledge in practical ways (medicine, engineering, navigation)

### Moral Choices & Consequences
- Major decisions affect both personal story and historical events
- Meaningful religious choices without "good/evil" simplification
- Complex loyalty systems reflecting the era's cultural and political dynamics

### Lifestyle Mechanics
- Build and manage property (from modest workshops to palaces depending on status)
- Participate in cultural traditions (poetry contests, festivals, pilgrimages)
- Form deep relationships (mentorships, rivalries, romances, families)

## Story Structure

### Main Questlines
Multiple main storylines available based on character background and choices:
- **Path of Power** - Rise through political or military ranks to influence history
- **Path of Wisdom** - Pursue knowledge and preserve learning during turbulent times
- **Path of Faith** - Navigate religious complexities as faiths compete and coexist
- **Path of Fortune** - Build wealth and connections across divided lands

### Historical Events
Major historical events serve as narrative anchors, with player actions potentially changing outcomes:
- **Battle of Las Navas de Tolosa** (1212)
- **Founding of the Nasrid Dynasty** (1230s)
- **The Black Death's arrival** (1340s)
- **Fall of Granada** (1492)

### Personal Storylines
Character-driven narratives exploring themes of:
- Identity in a multicultural society
- Loyalty when communities are divided
- Faith in an era of religious complexity
- Legacy in changing times

## Visual & Audio Design

### Art Direction
- Historically accurate architectural environments (Moorish, Visigothic, Romanesque, Mudéjar)
- Character designs reflecting the diverse ethnic and religious appearances of the period
- Interface inspired by Islamic geometric patterns and medieval manuscript illumination

### Music & Sound
- Authentic Andalusian musical traditions
- Voice acting in multiple languages (Arabic, medieval Spanish dialects, Latin)
- Environmental soundscapes for bustling medinas, Christian monasteries, and rural villages

## Key Features Summary
- Deep character customization reflecting the diverse populations of medieval Iberia
- Morally complex storytelling exploring a fascinating but underrepresented historical period
- Skill-based progression system emphasizing learning through practice
- Rich world-building combining historical accuracy with compelling gameplay
- Multiple narrative paths allowing for vastly different experiences across playthroughs
